Privacy policy

YouTube Export is a personal tool used only by its owner, Kent Løset.

  • It signs in with Google and reads YouTube data from the signed-in account only, such as liked videos and playlists. It uses read-only access.
  • The data is saved on the owner's own devices and is not sold or shared with anyone.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
